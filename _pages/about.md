I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering and Utah Water Research Laboratory at Utah State University. I am a fluvial geomorphologist interested in the impacts of perturbations on river systems, such as floods, land use change, and climate variability. I currently work in the Sediment Dynamics Group at the Utah Water Research Laboratory, where I combine fieldwork, data analysis, and modeling to better understand sediment transport and channel evolution.

## Education

- PhD Civil & Environmental Engineering, Utah State University, 2024 - Present
- MSc Geology, Utah State University, 2023
- BS Geology, Northern Arizona University, 2019
